# Figure legends

**Figure 1. Discrimination across validation designs.** (a) Record-pooled AUROC for SGD logistic regression and LightGBM under row-random, learner-grouped, learner-plus-class and learner-plus-teacher validation. (b) Record-pooled AUROC for standard leave-one-experiment-out (LOEO), training-size-matched LOEO, learner-purged LOEO and joint learner/class/teacher purging. (c) Median paired, experiment-level LightGBM-minus-SGD-logistic AUROC differences with 95% intervals from 10,000 experiment-level bootstrap resamples across 80 AUROC-eligible experiments. Pooled AUROC differences and medians of experiment-level AUROC differences are distinct statistics.

**Figure 2. Predictor sets and contextual-feature ablation.** (a) Experiment-level median AUROC with 95% experiment-bootstrap intervals for SGD logistic regression with complete learner history or the full predictor set, alongside the full-feature L-BFGS sensitivity fit. The annotation gives the paired learner-history-minus-full-set difference for the original SGD logistic-regression comparator. (b) Median paired AUROC change after removing coarse context under row-random and learner-purged LOEO, with 95% experiment-bootstrap intervals. The cross-design context-effect contrast is shown separately. All comparisons use the shared eligible target experiments in each pair.

**Figure 3. Calibration and chronological local updating.** (a) Experiment-level calibration intercept and slope medians and interquartile ranges across 70 calibration-eligible experiments for the SGD logistic-regression, L-BFGS logistic-regression and LightGBM models. Horizontal reference lines denote ideal intercept 0 and slope 1. (b) Mean experiment-level Brier scores and 95% experiment-bootstrap intervals among 69 eligible experiments for SGD logistic regression, L-BFGS logistic regression, LightGBM and the prevalence-only reference, without local updating, using the calibration cohort's observed event prevalence, and after chronological local intercept or intercept-plus-slope updating. Smaller Brier scores indicate better probability accuracy. Intercept-only updating improved Brier score in 55/69 experiments for SGD logistic regression, 50/69 for L-BFGS logistic regression and 52/69 for LightGBM; it worsened in 14/69, 19/69 and 17/69 experiments, respectively. Experiment-specific changes are listed in the local-recalibration result tables. All methods were evaluated on matched later records after the calibration outcome-maturation interval.

**Figure 4. Capacity-constrained endpoint selection.** (a,b) Record-pooled precision and recall at six within-experiment review capacities (5–30%), for the original SGD logistic regression, an L-BFGS sensitivity fit and LightGBM, with pointwise 95% experiment-cluster bootstrap intervals. (c,d) Paired LightGBM-minus-SGD-logistic and LightGBM-minus-L-BFGS precision and recall differences with corresponding pointwise 95% experiment-bootstrap intervals. No multiplicity adjustment was applied across capacities or endpoints. The y-axis ranges in panels (a) and (b) are restricted to improve visual resolution; panels (c) and (d) show paired differences relative to zero.

**Supplementary Figure S1. Outcome-horizon sensitivity.** AUROC under five-fold experiment-blocked, learner-purged validation for 7-day, 14-day, 30-day and archive-closure recorded noncompletion outcomes. The figure compares the original SGD logistic-regression and LightGBM specifications.

**Supplementary Figure S2. Repeated-learner sensitivity.** AUROC under learner-purged LOEO when each learner's total evaluation weight is balanced and when evaluation is restricted to that learner's earliest eligible record. Lines connect the two models evaluated on the same sensitivity subset.
